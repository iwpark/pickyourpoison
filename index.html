Pick Your Poison

Choose a model. Every other model tells you what's wrong with it, and it gets to answer for itself.

How it works

  1. Every model, including the one being examined, gets the same prompt, so differences in tone come from the model and not the question.
  2. A judge model from a different company merges items that describe the same controversy, then two judges from other companies rate each one from 1 (minor) to 5 (severe). Their scores are averaged, so no company grades its own homework.
  3. Raised by 3 or more rivals: Widely reported. Raised by one rival: Unverified. Models can be wrong or invent things, so treat everything here as an allegation to check.
  4. Harshness = the total severity of the controversies a model raised about a target. Refusals are recorded, not hidden.
  5. Results are cached in data/results.json. Refresh live reruns the queries through Hopscotch (about 54 API calls).
